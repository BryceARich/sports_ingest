### Added

- `SportsIngest::NHL.schedule`: NHL games by date (`date:`), date range (`from:`, `to:`), or team
  and season (`team:`, `season:`), as `NHL::Game` models with both sides as `NHL::GameTeam`;
  `team:` also filters a date or range. Adds `NHL::Client` for the NHL web API. ADR 0017 (M2-02).
