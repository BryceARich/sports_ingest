### Added

- `SportsIngest::NHL.schedule`: NHL games by date (`date:`), date range (`from:`, `to:`), or team
  and season or month (`team:` with `season:` or `month:`), as `NHL::Game` models with both sides
  as `NHL::GameTeam`; `team:` also narrows a date or range, read from that team's own weekly
  schedule. NHL codes the gem doesn't know read as `:unknown` / `:other`
  with the raw code in `Game#codes` and a warning. Adds `NHL::Client` for the NHL web API. ADR 0017 (M2-02).
