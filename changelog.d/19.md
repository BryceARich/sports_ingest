### Added

- NHL live game state: `NHL::GameScore` gains `home_shots` / `away_shots` (shots on goal),
  `situation` (an `NHL::Situation`: skaters per side, goalies in or pulled, penalty time left)
  with `power_play` (`:home`, `:away` or `nil`), and `phase` (`:pre`, `:regulation`,
  `:intermission`, `:overtime`, `:shootout`, `:final`, or `:unknown`), all from the same `/score`
  request. ADR 0019 (M2-04).

### Changed

- `NHL::GameScore` is schema 2, so cached scoreboards from earlier versions are refetched (M2-04).
