### Added

- The shared HTTP client, `SportsIngest::HTTP::Client`, on Net::HTTP: GET with timeouts,
  retries with exponential backoff and jitter on 408/429/5xx and network errors (honouring
  `Retry-After`), per-host rate limits shared across clients (`HTTP::RateLimiter`), the
  configured user agent, gzip, redirects, and errors mapped to `AuthError`, `NotFound`,
  `HTTPError` and `ParseError`. ADR 0008 (M1-05).
- WebMock as a development dependency; specs can no longer reach the network (M1-05).
