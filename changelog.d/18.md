### Added

- NHL scores: `SportsIngest::NHL.scores` (or `NHL::Scoreboard#on`) returns all of today's games,
  by the NHL's date, in one request to `/score/now`, or one day's with `date:`. Each is an
  `NHL::GameScore`: the `NHL::Game` with scores and state, plus period, period type and clock.
  The default client keeps scores fresh in the raw cache for 30 seconds (`Scoreboard::TTL`), and
  `NHL::Client.new` takes `ttl:` for that. ADR 0018 (M2-03).
