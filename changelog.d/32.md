### Added

- `SportsIngest::ESPN::Client`, the read-only ESPN Fantasy client for hockey (`fhl`) and football
  (`ffl`): `season`, `league` and general `get` / `get_json` with `view=` parameters, the
  `espn_s2` / `SWID` cookies from configuration, raw caching, and an `AuthError` that says which
  cookies are missing or that they may have expired. ADR 0032 (M4-01).
- `SportsIngest::ESPN::Filter`, which builds the `X-Fantasy-Filter` header (M4-01).
