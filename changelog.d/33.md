### Added

- `SportsIngest::ESPN::LeagueSettings`, which reads a league's `mSettings` and `mTeam` views into a
  plain Hash: scoring as stat ID => points, per-slot points (D/ST tiers), reverse (lower is better)
  stats, roster slot counts and teams with their owners' display names. ADR 0033 (M4-02).
- `SportsIngest::ESPN::StatIds` and `SportsIngest::ESPN::LineupSlots`, the football and hockey
  stat-ID and lineup-slot dictionaries, checked against ESPN's league defaults and real season
  stats (M4-02).
