### Added

- Licensed-data guard: `script/licensed-data-guard` refuses data files that look like
  Evolving-Hockey or PuckPedia exports (by name, or an `EH_ID` column or source name in the
  content), except invented-value fixtures in `spec/fixtures/advanced_hockey/`. It runs as a
  pre-commit hook (`.githooks/`, turned on by `bin/setup`) and as a CI job over tracked files and
  every commit in a PR. `/data/` and `/exports/` are gitignored. ADR 0014 (M1-11).
