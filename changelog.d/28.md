### Added

- `SportsIngest::Identity::Matcher`: resolves a source's player record to a canonical player in
  tiers (source ID, name and birth date, name with position and team, unique name) and returns a
  `SportsIngest::Identity::Match` with the ref, a confidence level, the reason, and the candidates
  when it can't decide. Both Elias Petterssons and both Sebastian Ahos resolve. ADR 0028 (M3-03).
