### Added

- `SportsIngest::Teams` and `SportsIngest::Team`: the 32 NHL teams with IDs, tri-codes, names,
  conference, division and colors, resolvable from NHL, ESPN, advanced-stats (`:advanced_stats`)
  and salary cap (`:salary_cap`) codes (`Teams.fetch("T.B")`) or IDs
  (`Teams.find_by_source_id(:espn, 20)`). ADR 0016 (M2-01).

### Changed

- Recorded cassettes store response bodies as text, with JSON pretty-printed, instead of base64
  when a body holds non-ASCII characters, so re-recorded fixtures diff field by field (M2-01).
