### Added

- NHL season stat tables: `SportsIngest::NHL.skater_stats` / `.goalie_stats` (and
  `NHL::SeasonStats#skaters` / `#goalies`) read the stats REST API's `skater/summary` and
  `goalie/summary` tables into `NHL::SeasonTotal` rows (NHL player ID, name, position, teams, and
  an `NHL::StatLine`), one per player per season, for `seasons: [...]` or `last: N` (default 3),
  regular season or playoffs. Each season is saved to the processed cache. ADR 0024 (M2-09).
- `SportsIngest::Seasons.resolve`, the shared `seasons:` / `last:` helper ADR 0006 called for, and
  `NHL::Season.current(date)`, which turns the season over on October 1 (M2-09).
