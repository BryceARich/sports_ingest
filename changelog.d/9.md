### Added

- The raw cache tier, `SportsIngest::Cache::Raw`: wraps `HTTP::Client`, keys responses by
  source, URL, params and headers (secrets only hashed), serves them while younger than the
  source's TTL, and serves the stored copy marked `stale` when the network fails (no response,
  408, 429 or 5xx). Stores: `Cache::MemoryStore`, `Cache::DiskStore` (JSON files under
  `<cache_dir>/raw`, owner-only) and `Cache::NullStore`. ADR 0009 (M1-06).
- `Configuration#raw_cache` (`SPORTS_INGEST_RAW_CACHE`): `disk` (default), `memory` or `off` (M1-06).
- `HTTP::Response#fetched_at` and `#stale` / `#stale?` (M1-06).
