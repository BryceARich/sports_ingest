### Added

- `NHL::Client#stats_json` for the NHL stats REST API (`api.nhle.com/stats/rest/en`), and
  `web` / `web_response` alongside `get_json` (M2-06).
- `SportsIngest::NHL::Standings.fetch(date:)`, the league standings on a date as models:
  `Standings` (date, season, update time, `find`, `division`, `conference`) and one `Standing` per
  team, with ranks, wild-card place, clinch letter, totals and `home` / `road` / `last_10`
  `Record`s. ADR 0021 (M2-06).
