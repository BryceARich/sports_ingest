### Added

- `SportsIngest::Identity::UnmatchedReport`: after matching a batch of source records, lists the
  ones that need a look (unmatched, ambiguous, or matched only on a unique name) with the players
  they could be, as console text (`#to_text`) or CSV (`#to_csv`). ADR 0030 (M3-05).
- `SportsIngest::AdvancedHockey::SkaterGarImport.report(path, matcher:, io:)`: imports an EH skater
  GAR table, matches each player, and prints the report. A traded player is matched without a team.
  ADR 0030 (M3-05).
