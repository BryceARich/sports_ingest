### Added

- nflverse player stats: `SportsIngest::Nflverse::PlayerStats#weekly` and `#season` load weekly
  game lines (`PlayerWeekStats`) and regular-season totals (`PlayerSeasonStats`) from the
  `stats_player` release, for `seasons:` or `last:` (default 3). Stats are numeric columns keyed by
  their nflverse names; blank and `NA` cells are left out. Each season is saved to the processed
  cache. ADR 0041 (M5-02).
