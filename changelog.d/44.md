### Added

- Evolving-Hockey skater GAR import: `SportsIngest::AdvancedHockey::SkaterGarImport.read(path)`
  reads a manually exported CSV into `SkaterGar` models, row for row, with seasons as start years
  and multi-team cells such as `VAN/MIN` split into `teams`. Columns match by name; missing columns
  or bad cells raise `SourceChangedError`. Adds the pure-Ruby `csv` gem dependency. ADR 0044 (M6-01).
