### Added

- Evolving-Hockey skater GAR import: `SportsIngest::AdvancedHockey::SkaterGarImport.read(path)`
  reads EH's skater GAR CSV, grouped by season or by team and season, into `SkaterGar` models,
  row for row, with seasons as start years and multi-team cells such as `N.J/WPG` split into
  `teams`. `SkaterGar.combine(rows)` merges team rows into one row per player and season. Columns
  match by name; missing columns or bad cells raise `SourceChangedError`. Adds the pure-Ruby `csv`
  gem dependency. ADR 0044 (M6-01).
