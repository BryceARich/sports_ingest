### Added

- Raw cache eviction: entries older than the source's eviction age (default 10 × its TTL) are
  deleted and never served, even stale. Set it with `evict_after` / `default_evict_after` or
  `SPORTS_INGEST_EVICT_AFTER_<SOURCE>` / `SPORTS_INGEST_EVICT_AFTER`; 0 means never evict (M1-13).
- `Cache::Raw#prune` deletes every expired entry in the store, including ones saved under old ESPN
  cookies; `#get` runs it after a write at most once an hour. Stores gain `#keys` (M1-13).

### Changed

- A stale raw-cache copy is only served within the eviction age, no longer however old (M1-13).
