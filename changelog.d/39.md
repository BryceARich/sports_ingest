### Changed

- ESPN reads moved under `SportsIngest::ESPN::Read`: `Client`, `Filter`, `Drafts`, `Draft` and
  `DraftPick` (ADR 0039, M4-08). The old `SportsIngest::ESPN::*` names are gone.

### Added

- `SportsIngest::ESPN::Write` is reserved for future ESPN writes, not implemented. It loads only
  when named (ADR 0039, M4-08).
